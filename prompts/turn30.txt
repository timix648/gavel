Turn 30: one safety fix in src/pages/Create.tsx. Change nothing else.
erc20RawAmount uses `parseTokenAmount(tokenAmountStr, tokenDecimals ?? 18)`. Before decimals() loads, "17" becomes 17e18 units, which for EURC (6 decimals) is a trillion times too large, and the wallet would be asked to approve it.
1. If tokenDecimals is undefined, make erc20RawAmount 0n (no 18 fallback).
2. In the submit validation, for ERC-20 lots: if tokenDecimals or tokenBalance is undefined, setError("Still reading the token from the chain. Try again in a moment.") and return, before any approval or list call.
