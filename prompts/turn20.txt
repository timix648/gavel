1. /lots NEVER LOADS (top priority). The page stays on skeletons forever, both before and
   after connecting a wallet. The browser console shows 351 errors, nearly all HTTP 429
   from https://rpc.testnet.arc.io. The lobby's lots multicall fails, the catch keeps the
   skeleton, and nothing retries with backoff or tells the user.
   a) RPC load: in src/config.ts use http(url, { batch: { wait: 20 }, retryCount: 4,
      retryDelay: 1500 }) so concurrent reads go out as ONE JSON-RPC batch, and set the
      wagmi/viem pollingInterval to 4000 ms. useBlock / watchBlocks must not poll faster
      than that. Throttle the lobby and landing house-lot refresh to at most once per
      4 s (not every block) and pause all polling while document.hidden.
   b) Resilience: reuse logStore's rate-limit backoff for the lobby and landing multicalls
      (same -32005 / 429 detection, exponential delay). While a first load is failing,
      replace the skeleton after ~6 s with an inline line "The public Arc RPC is busy.
      Retrying in Ns" with a live countdown, and keep retrying. Never sit on skeletons
      silently.
   c) RPC override for production: read VITE_RPC_URL (a provider URL) first, then fall
      back to the public RPC, in src/chain.ts. Document it in README.
