Gasless bidding currently makes the bidder sign TWICE (USDC ReceiveWithAuthorization +
our BidAuthorization). Reduce it to ONE signature with the same security. Balanced preset.

Design: bind the lot inside the EIP-3009 nonce itself.
- New signature: bidWithAuthorization(uint256 lotId, uint256 amount, uint256 validAfter,
  uint256 validBefore, bytes32 salt, uint8 v, bytes32 r, bytes32 s). Read `from` by
  recovering nothing ourselves - instead take `address from` as a parameter too.
- The contract computes nonce = keccak256(abi.encode(address(this), block.chainid, lotId,
  amount, salt)) and calls USDC.receiveWithAuthorization(from, address(this), amount,
  validAfter, validBefore, nonce, v, r, s).
- Because USDC verifies the signature over that exact nonce, a relayer cannot change
  lotId, amount, chain or contract without invalidating it, and receiveWithAuthorization
  (msg.sender must equal `to`) stops it being replayed as a plain transfer. USDC's own
  used-nonce tracking prevents replay.
- Delete the BidAuthorization struct, its typehash, the ECDSA recovery and our domain
  separator code - no longer needed.
- Frontend: the user signs ONE ReceiveWithAuthorization typed-data message whose nonce is
  computed client-side with the same formula (random salt). Relayer API: {lotId, amount,
  from, validAfter, validBefore, salt, sig}; relayer recomputes the nonce and checks it.
