Turn 8b - fixes from my browser review of the redesign (1280 px and 375 px). Quick
preset, frontend only, keep everything else as is.

1. MOBILE ORDER on /lot/:id: at < 768 px the auction panel (clock, bid, Bid button) must
   come directly after the title/seller, BEFORE the image and bid history. It is the
   primary action; right now a phone user has to scroll past the image to find it.
2. The fixed "Built with Arc Studio" badge (position: fixed bottom-right, inline styles,
   light blue) covers the mobile tab bar's Me and Proof items and breaks the one-accent
   rule. Remove the fixed badge. Put "Built with Arc Studio" as a small text link in a
   footer on desktop and at the end of the page content on mobile, styled with tokens
   (--ink-3, accent on hover). No inline styles.
3. Fonts: Geist Sans 700 is not imported (headings are being faux-bolded). Import the 700
   weight. Also the CSS font-family lists "Geist" first but the loaded family is
   "Geist Sans" - put the correct loaded family name first.
4. /lot/:id panel shows the reserve twice ("Reserve $0.10" as the big figure AND again in
   the small line). When there are no bids, show the big figure labelled "Reserve" and
   only "Min step $0.05" in the small line.
5. Lobby with no live lot: there is a large empty vertical gap between the hero and the
   "No live auction right now" state. Tighten it: the empty state sits directly under the
   hero with normal section spacing, and the hero's house-lot panel shows the most recent
   settled house lot ("Last sold: UI wireframe, no bids") instead of only "Next house lot
   starts in a moment".
6. Lot image: when lotURI has no image, do not use a random picsum photo (a random photo
   for "Logo sketch" is misleading). Show a composed placeholder panel instead: the lot
   title large in --ink-3 on --surface with the Phosphor Gavel icon. Use the image field
   from data:application/json lotURIs when present.
7. Per-digit roll for the current bid is optional; leave the fade-up.
