Turn 6 - the always-on house auction + UI polish. Quick preset for review. Keep it
focused; do not change contracts.

A. HOUSE AUCTION KEEPER - a small Bun service in keeper/ (separate from the app and the
   relayer), so a reviewer can bid on a live lot at any time:
   - Every cycle: if no house lot is live, list a new one from the house wallet
     (HOUSE_PRIVATE_KEY env, never logged): title from a rotating list of digital lots
     (e.g. "30-min code review", "Logo sketch", "Custom avatar", "Domain name idea",
     "Shout-out post"), reserve 0.10 USDC, minIncrement 0.05, duration 120 s; image URL
     optional.
   - When a house lot's deadline passes, call settle() (anyone can), then for lots with a
     winner call confirmDelivery is NOT the keeper's job - leave it to the winner; but
     after the 7-day timeout call releaseBondAfterTimeout for old house lots.
   - Approve USDC for the bond once with a sensible allowance; check balance before each
     list and log a clear warning below 2 USDC.
   - maxFeePerGas >= 20 gwei, maxPriorityFeePerGas 0; one tx in flight; exponential
     backoff on RPC -32005 like logStore; never crash-loop.
   - Logs one line per action with tx hash; GET /health returns {lastAction, liveLotId,
     balance}.
   - README section: env vars, how to run, how to host (e.g. a free worker/VM), cost per
     day estimate (gas per list + settle x cycles per day).
   - Run it in the sandbox for at least two full cycles against testnet and paste the log.
